Interactive notes for machine learning. Each topic pairs a short explanation with widgets you can poke at: change a hyperparameter, step through an algorithm, and watch what happens.

## Where to go

- **Foundations**: the math and data basics everything else builds on.
- **Supervised learning**: nearest neighbours, decision trees, and more.
- **Gallery**: every widget on one page.
