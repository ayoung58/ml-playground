Foundations will cover the notation and ideas used throughout the course.

Inline math: the squared error of a prediction $\hat{y}$ is $(y - \hat{y})^2$.

Block math:

$$
\mathrm{MSE} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \left(y_i - \hat{y}_i\right)^2
$$
